# Riveted Construction Manual
## OSEP-16 Aluminum Proa - Step-by-Step Build Guide

**Read this entire manual before starting construction.**

---

## BUILD OVERVIEW

**Total time:** 55 hours over 4 weekends  
**Skill level:** Beginner (no welding required)  
**Tools needed:** See BOM  
**Workspace:** 20' × 10' minimum (garage, driveway, barn)

---

## SAFETY FIRST

### Required Safety Equipment

- ✅ Safety glasses (ANSI Z87.1)
- ✅ Work gloves (leather or kevlar)
- ✅ Hearing protection (if using power tools)
- ✅ Steel-toed boots
- ✅ Dust mask (for metal dust)
- ✅ Fire extinguisher (ABC type, for plasma cutter)

### Shop Safety Rules

1. **No aluminum dust near flames** - aluminum dust is flammable
2. **Deburr immediately** - sharp edges cause cuts
3. **Ventilate when using sealant** - 3M 5200 fumes are strong
4. **Support panels** - unsupported aluminum flexes and can pinch
5. **Check rivet gun** - bucking bar can slip and strike fingers

---

## PHASE 1: PANEL CUTTING (Day 1, 3 hours)

### Step 1.1: CNC Plasma Cut All Panels

**Equipment:** CNC plasma cutter (see CNC-PLASMA-SETUP.md)

**Process:**
1. Load DXF files from /cad/ directory
2. Position aluminum sheet on CNC bed
3. Secure with clamps or magnets
4. Set torch height: 0.150" (3.8mm)
5. Cut speed: 60 IPM for 1/8" aluminum
6. Amperage: 30A (for thin aluminum)

**Panels to cut:**
- Main hull bottom (1)
- Main hull sides (2) - **mark PORT and STARBOARD**
- Bow triangle (1)
- Stern triangle (1)
- Bulkheads (4) - **mark positions: STA 2, 4, 6, 8**
- Ama panels (5) - **same as hull, 75% scale**

**Time:** 2-3 hours for all panels

**Quality check:**
- ✅ All panels cut completely through
- ✅ Minimal dross (slag) on bottom edge
- ✅ No warping from heat

---

### Step 1.2: Deburr All Edges

**Tool:** Hand deburring tool or file

**Process:**
1. Run deburr tool along EVERY cut edge
2. Both sides of panel
3. Pay extra attention to rivet hole areas
4. Smooth any plasma spatter

**Time:** 1 hour

**Why:** Sharp edges cut hands, prevent good seals, and look unprofessional

**Quality check:**
- ✅ Can run finger along edge without cuts
- ✅ No burrs visible
- ✅ Edges smooth and consistent

---

### Step 1.3: Mark Rivet Locations

**Tools:** Marker, ruler, tape measure

**Process:**
1. Lay panels on flat surface
2. Mark overlap zones (1/2" to 3/4" from edge)
3. Mark rivet positions every 1.5-2" along seam
4. Use staggered double-row pattern (see diagram below)

**Staggered Pattern:**
```
Edge    ●   ●   ●   ●   ●   Row 1 (1/2" from edge)
        
         ●   ●   ●   ●    Row 2 (1" from edge, offset)
```

**Panels to mark:**
- Bottom panel: Mark where sides will overlap
- Side panels: Mark keel and chine overlap zones
- Bow/stern: Mark where all panels meet

**Time:** 30 minutes

**Quality check:**
- ✅ Rivet spacing consistent (1.5-2")
- ✅ Rows staggered (not aligned)
- ✅ Edge distance minimum 1/2"

---

## PHASE 2: HULL ASSEMBLY (Days 2-3, 13 hours)

### Step 2.1: Bend Chines (if not pre-bent)

**Tool:** Sheet metal brake OR improvised bender

**Process:**

**Option A: Sheet metal brake (recommended)**
1. Mark bend line on side panels (centerline)
2. Position panel in brake at 120° angle
3. Clamp and pull handle to bend
4. Check angle with protractor

**Option B: Improvised farm equipment method**
1. Clamp panel between two 2x4s along bend line
2. Use tractor bucket or come-along to apply even pressure
3. Bend slowly, checking angle frequently
4. Final angle: 120° (chine angle)

**Time:** 2 hours (both side panels)

**Quality check:**
- ✅ Bend is straight (no kinks)
- ✅ Angle is 120° ± 5°
- ✅ No cracks in aluminum at bend

---

### Step 2.2: Dry-Fit Hull

**Purpose:** Check fit before permanent assembly

**Process:**
1. Lay bottom panel flat on worktable
2. Position port side panel, align with marks
3. Position starboard side panel
4. Clamp with spring clamps every 12"
5. Check that bow and stern triangles fit
6. Adjust panel positions as needed

**Time:** 1 hour

**Quality check:**
- ✅ All panels meet at corners
- ✅ Gaps between panels < 1/8"
- ✅ Hull is symmetrical (port = starboard)
- ✅ Bottom is flat (no twist)

**Common issues:**
- Gap too large? Sand/file high spots
- Panel doesn't meet? Re-check chine bend angle
- Twisted? Adjust clamp positions

---

### Step 2.3: Drill Rivet Holes

**Tools:** Cordless drill, #30 bit (0.128" for 1/8" rivets)

**Process:**
1. Starting at bow, drill through BOTH overlapped panels
2. Use pilot marks from Step 1.3
3. Drill perpendicular to panel surface
4. Deburr each hole immediately
5. Work toward stern, drilling every marked rivet position

**Important:** Keep panels clamped while drilling to prevent shifting

**Time:** 2 hours (all keel and chine seams)

**Quality check:**
- ✅ Holes are round (not oblong)
- ✅ Holes align through both panels
- ✅ Hole size: 1/8" rivet fits snugly
- ✅ All holes deburred

**Troubleshooting:**
- Drill walking? Use center punch first
- Holes misaligned? Re-clamp panels tighter
- Bit breaking? Use cutting oil, slower speed

---

### Step 2.4: Wet-Rivet Keel Seam (CRITICAL STEP)

**This is where waterproofing happens. Take your time.**

**Materials:** 3M 5200 sealant, rivets, rivet gun, bucking bar, acetone, rags

**Process:**

**A. Prepare surfaces**
1. Wipe all surfaces with acetone (removes oil)
2. Let dry 5 minutes
3. Lay bottom panel flat

**B. Apply sealant to seam**
1. Run 1/4" bead of 5200 along keel edge of bottom panel
2. Apply to full length of seam

**C. Position side panel**
1. Place port side panel on bottom panel
2. Align drilled holes
3. Clamp every 8-12"

**D. Apply sealant to holes**
1. Using finger or small brush, dab sealant around EACH hole
2. On both sides of joint
3. Sealant should be visible in hole

**E. Set rivets**
1. Insert rivet through sealant-coated hole
2. Rivet will squeeze sealant as it goes through (messy - this is good!)
3. Place bucking bar on inside (back) of rivet
4. Position rivet gun on head (front)
5. Squeeze trigger firmly - rivet will expand with loud POP
6. Rivet is set when head is flush and tail is mushroomed
7. Wipe excess sealant with rag
8. Move to next rivet

**F. Seal rivet heads**
1. After all rivets set, apply small dab of 5200 over each head
2. Smooth with finger
3. Creates final gasket

**Time:** 4 hours (keel seam)

**Quality check:**
- ✅ Sealant squeeze-out visible between panels
- ✅ Each rivet head is flush
- ✅ Tail is mushroomed (visible from inside)
- ✅ Rivet doesn't spin when touched
- ✅ Sealant dab covers each rivet head

**Troubleshooting:**
- Rivet spinning? Not fully set - hit again harder
- No squeeze-out? Not enough sealant - add more before next rivet
- Rivet crooked? Drill out, start over with new rivet

---

### Step 2.5: Wet-Rivet Chine Seams

**Repeat Step 2.4 process for:**
1. Port chine (where side meets bottom)
2. Starboard chine

**Time:** 3 hours each side = 6 hours total

**Note:** Work in sections - don't try to rivet entire seam in one session (sealant has 30-45 min working time)

---

### Step 2.6: Attach Bow and Stern Panels

**Process:**
1. Trim bow/stern triangles if needed for final fit
2. Drill holes through triangle into hull sides
3. Wet-rivet using same process as Step 2.4
4. May need helper to hold triangular panel in position

**Time:** 1 hour

---

### Step 2.7: Let Cure

**Critical:** Allow 24-48 hours for 5200 to cure before moving hull

**During cure time:**
- Check for any sealant drips, wipe away before fully cured
- Inspect all rivets for proper setting
- Plan bulkhead installation

---

## PHASE 3: BULKHEADS & FLOOD TEST (Day 4, 4 hours)

### Step 3.1: Install Bulkheads

**Bulkhead positions:** Stations 2, 4, 6, 8 (marked on panels)

**Process:**
1. Slide bulkhead into hull from open stern
2. Position at station mark
3. Check bulkhead is perpendicular with square
4. Clamp in position
5. Drill holes through hull into bulkhead edge (single row, 2" spacing)
6. Rivet bulkhead to hull (can be dry-riveted - not underwater)

**Repeat for all 4 bulkheads**

**Time:** 2 hours

**Quality check:**
- ✅ Bulkheads perpendicular to keel
- ✅ Bulkheads at correct stations
- ✅ All rivets secure

---

### Step 3.2: Flood Test (CRITICAL QUALITY CHECK)

**Purpose:** Find leaks BEFORE launch

**Process:**
1. Plug drain hole (if any) with tape
2. Place hull right-side-up on sawhorses
3. Fill hull with water using hose (6" depth)
4. Let sit for 24 hours
5. Inspect underneath for drips

**What to look for:**
- Drips from specific rivets
- Seepage along seams
- Wet spots on outside of hull

**Mark any leaks with marker**

**Time:** 30 minutes active, 24 hours passive

---

### Step 3.3: Fix Any Leaks

**If you find leaks (1-3% of rivets typical):**

1. Empty and dry hull completely
2. Identify leaking rivet from outside
3. Apply dab of 5200 over rivet head
4. Let cure 24 hours
5. Re-test

**If >10% of rivets leak:** Major issue - may need to drill out section and re-rivet with more sealant

**Time:** 1 hour + 24hr cure

---

## PHASE 4: BUILD AMA (Days 5-6, 12 hours)

### Step 4.1: Repeat Hull Process for Ama

**Ama is same geometry as hull, just 75% scale**

**Process:**
1. Cut ama panels (already done in Phase 1)
2. Bend chines (same 120° angle)
3. Dry-fit
4. Drill rivet holes
5. Wet-rivet all seams
6. Install bulkheads (2 bulkheads in ama)
7. Flood test

**Time:** 12 hours (faster than hull because smaller and you have practice)

**Quality check:** Same as hull

---

## PHASE 5: CROSSBEAMS (AKAS) (Day 7, 5 hours)

### Step 5.1: Cut and Prepare Crossbeam Tubes

**Material:** 2" aluminum square tube, 48" length

**Process:**
1. Cut two tubes to 48" (if not already cut)
2. Deburr ends
3. Drill mounting holes at ends (4 holes per tube)

**Time:** 1 hour

---

### Step 5.2: Fabricate Pylon Mounts

**Purpose:** Attach crossbeams to hull

**Method A: Riveted tabs**
1. Cut aluminum plate into 4" × 4" squares (8 total - 4 per side)
2. Bend into U-shape to fit over hull chine
3. Drill holes for crossbeam bolts
4. Rivet tabs to hull at Stations 4 and 6

**Method B: Through-bolt (stronger)**
1. Drill through hull at Stations 4 and 6
2. Install backing plates inside hull
3. Bolt crossbeam directly through hull with 5/16" bolts

**Time:** 3 hours

---

### Step 5.3: Attach Crossbeams

**Process:**
1. Position hull and ama 4 feet apart
2. Bolt front crossbeam (Station 4) to hull and ama
3. Bolt rear crossbeam (Station 6) to hull and ama
4. Check that ama is parallel to hull
5. Tighten all bolts

**Time:** 1 hour

**Quality check:**
- ✅ Ama parallel to hull
- ✅ Crossbeams level
- ✅ All bolts tight
- ✅ Assembly is rigid

---

## FINAL STEPS

### Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] Flood test passed (no leaks)
- [ ] All rivets secure (none spin)
- [ ] All sharp edges deburred
- [ ] Mounting hardware installed
- [ ] Accessories attached
- [ ] Safety equipment on board (life jackets, whistle, paddle)
- [ ] Hull rinsed (remove metal dust, sealant residue)

### Launch Day!

1. Transport to water (cartop or trailer)
2. Inspect hull one more time
3. Carefully launch
4. Check for water intrusion after 1 hour
5. If dry: You built a boat! Go use it!
6. If minor seepage: Note location, fix with sealant later

## TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Problem: Rivet won't set properly

**Causes:**
- Bucking bar not positioned correctly
- Rivet gun not squeezed hard enough
- Wrong size rivet for hole

**Solution:**
- Drill out rivet
- Check hole size (should be snug fit)
- Try again with proper technique

---

### Problem: Panels don't align

**Causes:**
- Chine bend angle wrong
- Panels cut incorrectly
- Assembly sequence incorrect

**Solution:**
- Re-measure chine angle (should be 120°)
- Sand/file high spots
- Check build manual for correct order

---

### Problem: Flood test reveals multiple leaks

**Causes:**
- Insufficient sealant applied
- Panels not compressed during riveting
- Rivets not fully set

**Solution:**
- Apply sealant dabs over leaking rivet heads
- If >20 rivets leak: drill out section, re-rivet with more sealant
- Add epoxy fillet inside as secondary seal

---

### Problem: Hull twists or warps

**Causes:**
- Uneven clamping during assembly
- Bulkheads not square
- Bottom panel not flat during build

**Solution:**
- Remove bulkheads, re-install with square check
- May need to remove and re-position side panels
- Use jig or strongback to hold shape during assembly

---

## MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE

### After Each Use
- Rinse hull with fresh water
- Wipe down aluminum
- Check for new dents or damage

### Monthly
- Inspect rivet seams for weeping
- Touch up any seepage with 5200
- Check mounting hardware for looseness

### Annually
- Full inspection of all rivets
- Re-seal any persistent weepers
- Check bulkhead attachments

### Every 5-10 Years
- Expect to re-seal 10-20 rivets (normal)
- Drill out and replace any loose rivets

## TIPS FROM BUILDERS

💡 **"Practice riveting on scrap first"** - Set 10-20 practice rivets to get technique down

💡 **"Buy extra rivets"** - They're cheap, and you'll mess up a few

💡 **"Don't skip flood test"** - Fixing leaks on land is 100x easier than on water

💡 **"Work in sections"** - Don't try to rivet entire hull in one day (fatigue leads to mistakes)

💡 **"Get a helper"** - Bucking bar is easier with second person

💡 **"Take photos"** - Document each step for your own reference and to share
