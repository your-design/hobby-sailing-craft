## ORDERING STRATEGY

### Phase 1: Aluminum (Order First - 2 week lead time)

1. Call local metal supplier for 5052-H32 aluminum
2. Request quotes for sheets (often cheaper than online)
3. Ask about pre-bending chine angles ($50 upcharge usually worth it)
4. **Backup:** OnlineMetals.com ships nationwide

### Phase 2: Fasteners (Order Week 2)

1. McMaster-Carr for precision items (rivets, bolts)
2. Create account for volume pricing
3. Order all rivets + hardware in one shipment

### Phase 3: Mounting Hardware (Order Week 3)

1. Amazon Prime for quick delivery
2. West Marine for marine-specific items
3. Local hardware store for generic bolts/clamps

### Phase 4: Configuration-Specific (Order as needed)

1. Sailing rig: Duckworks Boat Builders Supply
2. Fishing gear: Amazon/local tackle shop  
3. Expedition: REI/outdoor retailer

## MONEY-SAVING TIPS

1. **Check metal scrap yards** - 5052 aluminum offcuts often 50% off
2. **Buy in bulk** - Order rivets by 100s from McMaster (unit price drops)
3. **Skip powder coating** - Bare aluminum oxidizes to attractive gray (free finish)
4. **DIY sail** - Sailrite kit saves $150 vs custom sail
5. **Borrow tools** - Rivet gun, clamps from friends or Harbor Freight rental
6. **Local hardware** - Bolts/washers cheaper at Ace vs McMaster
7. **Used rigging** - Check Craigslist for old Hobie Cat parts

**Potential savings:** $300-500
