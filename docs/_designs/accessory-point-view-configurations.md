# OSEP-16 Mounting Points and Accessories


## Configuration 1: Solo Fishing

Lightweight setup for single-person fly fishing and lake exploration. Features rod holders, paddle clips, anchor system, and fishfinder mount. Main hull only (no outrigger), easy cartop transport.

![Fishing Configuration](/assets/images/fishing-config-vintage.svg)

## Configuration 2: Family Day Sailing

Full proa assembly with crab claw sail rig. Includes mast step, shroud attachments, sheet blocks, tiller mount, and trampoline lacing. Stable platform for 2 adults + 1 child, cruising speeds 8-12 knots.

![Sailing Configuration](/assets/images/sailing-config-vintage.svg)

## Configuration 3: Expedition Camping

Extended cruising setup for 3-5 day self-sufficient trips. Combines all sailing hardware plus dry bag clips, water jug mount, tent pole sockets, camp stove gimbals, and 12 cu ft sealed ama storage. 50+ nautical mile range.

![Expedition Camping](/assets/images/expedition-config-vintage.svg)
