## COST-SAVING STRATEGIES

### 1. Buy Used
- **Sails:** Check Hobie fleet classifieds
- **Rigging:** Sailing forums often have free/cheap blocks
- **Aluminum:** Scrapyards sell offcuts cheap

### 2. Source Locally
- **Lumber:** Avoid shipping costs (plywood is HEAVY)
- **Hardware:** Local marine stores price-match online

### 3. Build in Winter
- **Off-season sales:** West Marine, Defender, etc.
- **Epoxy:** Doesn't spoil, buy when on sale

### 4. Tool Sharing
- **Borrow:** Friends, makerspaces, community colleges
- **Rent:** Home Depot rents sanders, saws for $20/day

### 5. Phased Building
- **Phase 1:** Hull only ($1,500)
- **Phase 2:** Add rig when budget allows ($1,200)
- **Phase 3:** Upgrades over time ($500+)
