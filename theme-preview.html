Design Philosophy

Hard-Chine Aluminum Construction

Unlike traditional plywood designs, our aluminum construction uses flat panels joined with solid rivets. This "hard-chine" approach creates sharp edges that generate turbulence for lateral resistance—just like an asymmetric hull.

Material: 5052-H32 marine-grade aluminum, 1/8" thick
Joints: 400 solid aluminum rivets with 3M 5200 marine sealant
Durability: 40+ year lifespan with zero maintenance

Three Use Configurations

Switch between fishing, sailing, and expedition modes in 15-20 minutes. The modular mounting system lets you adapt the boat to your current adventure.

Mode 1 Solo Fishing - Minimal setup, cartop-able

Mode 2 Family Sailing - Full rig with trampoline

Mode 3 Expedition - Camping gear + provisions

Build Process Overview

Weekend 1: Panel Cutting

  • CNC plasma cut all aluminum panels (3 hours)
  • Deburr edges and mark rivet locations (1 hour)
  • Assemble and wet-rivet main hull (12 hours)

Weekend 2: Ama & Testing

  1. Build ama outrigger using same process (12 hours)
  2. Conduct flood test on hull and ama (4 hours)
  3. Touch up any seeping rivets with sealant
