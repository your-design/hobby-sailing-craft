# EXPEDITION PROA - Technical Specifications

## Design Philosophy
**Name:** Open-Source Expedition Proa (OSEP-16)
**Design Type:** Modern Atlantic/Pacific Hybrid Proa
**Primary Use:** Multi-configuration lake sailing, fishing, expedition camping

---

## HULL SPECIFICATIONS

### Main Hull (Vaka)
- **Length Overall (LOA):** 16' 0" (4.88m)
- **Beam:** 24" (0.61m) at widest point
- **Draft:** 8" (0.20m) loaded
- **Hull Form:** Asymmetric
  - Leeward side: Flat with minimal rocker
  - Windward side: Rounded with moderate rocker
  - Both ends: Identical (shunting design)
- **Construction:** Stitch-and-glue plywood, 6mm marine ply
- **Weight (bare hull):** ~85 lbs (38.5 kg)
- **Displacement:** 450 lbs (204 kg) full load
- **Volume:** ~18 cubic feet sealed flotation

### Ama (Outrigger Float)
- **Length Overall:** 12' 0" (3.66m)
- **Beam:** 18" (0.46m)
- **Draft:** 6" (0.15m) loaded
- **Hull Form:** Symmetrical both ends, rounded bottom
- **Construction:** Stitch-and-glue plywood, 6mm marine ply
- **Weight:** ~35 lbs (16 kg)
- **Buoyancy:** 400 lbs (181 kg) reserve when sealed
- **Internal Volume:** 12 cubic feet (watertight storage)
- **Deck:** Removable hatch for gear access

### Akas (Crossbeams)
- **Configuration:** 2 crossbeams (forward & aft)
- **Length:** Adjustable 8' - 12' (2.44m - 3.66m)
- **Material:** 4" aluminum tube OR carbon fiber composite
- **Attachment:** Pylon mounts on main hull (similar to Hobie design)
- **Beam Width (effective):** 10' standard, 12' heavy weather
- **Trampoline Area:** 8' x 10' (2.44m x 3.05m)

---

## RIG SPECIFICATIONS

### Sail Plan (Primary Configuration)
- **Rig Type:** Unstayed mast with crab claw sail
- **Mast Height:** 20' (6.1m) from deck
- **Mast Material:** Aluminum or carbon fiber
- **Sail Area:** 140 sq ft (13 sq m) main
- **Sail Construction:** Dacron or modern laminate
- **Boom/Yard:** 14' curved yard (traditional crab claw)
- **Sheet System:** 4:1 continuous loop
- **Shunting:** Reversible sail, both ends functional

### Alternative Rig (Modern Variant)
- **Rig Type:** Gaff or lateen
- **Sail Area:** 160 sq ft (14.9 sq m)
- **Compatibility:** Sunfish/Laser-style simplification

---

## PERFORMANCE TARGETS

### Speed (Priority 3/5)
- **Light air (5-10 kt):** 3-5 knots
- **Moderate (10-15 kt):** 8-12 knots
- **Heavy air (15-20 kt):** 12-15 knots
- **Hull speed:** ~5.8 knots (theoretical displacement)
- **Planing capability:** Yes, in 15+ kt winds

### Stability (Priority 5/5)
- **Static stability:** Ama provides 400 lbs buoyancy reserve
- **Standing height:** 6' person can stand on trampoline or ama
- **Capsize resistance:** Significantly higher than Hobie 16
- **Righting:** Self-righting with ama flotation
- **Crew capacity:** 2 adults + 1 child (500 lbs total)

### Shallow Water (Priority 4/5)
- **Minimum operating depth:** 12" (0.30m)
- **Beaching:** Direct beach landing, no daggerboard to retract
- **Rudder:** Kick-up design (Hobie-style)
- **Groundings:** Asymmetric hull resists broaching

### Transport (Priority 3/5)
- **Trailer:** Fits 16' flatbed
- **Assembly time:** 45 minutes (ama attachment + rig)
- **Breakdown:** Ama and mast removable
- **Storage:** Main hull 16'x2', Ama 12'x1.5', Akas 12'
- **Top-loading:** Possible on standard roof rack (with ama separate)

---

## CONFIGURATIONS

### 1. Solo Fishing Mode
- **Rig:** Main hull only (NO ama attached)
- **Propulsion:** Paddle or small sail (reduce to 80 sq ft)
- **Stability:** Main hull acts as wide SOT kayak
- **Fishing platform:** Stand in hull, use flush-mount rod holders
- **Weight:** 85 lbs (hull only)
- **Transport:** Fits in pickup bed

### 2. Family Day Sailing
- **Rig:** Full proa (main hull + ama + full sail)
- **Crew:** 2 adults on trampoline, 1 child on ama or trampoline
- **Sailing:** Traditional shunting or modern tacking variant
- **Speed:** 8-12 knots typical
- **Safety:** High stability, low capsize risk

### 3. Expedition Camping
- **Rig:** Full proa with enclosed ama
- **Storage:** Ama holds 12 cu ft gear (sleeping bag, tent, food, water)
- **Camping:** Beach landing, sleep on trampoline or shore
- **Range:** 50+ miles per day in moderate wind
- **Provisions:** 3-5 day capacity for solo sailor

## MATERIALS & BUILD METHOD

### Primary Construction: Stitch-and-Glue
- **Panels:** 6mm Okoume or BS1088 marine plywood
- **Adhesive:** WEST System epoxy or equivalent
- **Reinforcement:** 6 oz fiberglass cloth (exterior)
- **Fasteners:** Copper wire (temporary), epoxy fillets (permanent)

### CNC Cut Files (4'x8' Optimization)
- **Main hull panels:** 8 panels (nested on 3 sheets)
- **Ama panels:** 6 panels (nested on 2 sheets)
- **Bulkheads:** 6 total (nested on 1 sheet)
- **Total plywood:** 6 sheets 4'x8' @ 6mm
- **Waste:** <15% with optimized nesting

### Hardware & Rigging
- **Akas:** Aluminum tube 4" diameter, 0.125" wall
- **Pylon mounts:** Stainless steel, through-bolted
- **Rudder system:** Hobie-style kick-up OR custom CNC cut
- **Sail:** Off-the-shelf Sunfish sail OR custom Dacron
- **Lines:** Standard marine rope, Harken blocks
- **Trampoline:** Mesh fabric with lacing system

## ADVANTAGES OVER HOBIE 16

| Feature | Hobie 16 | OSEP-16 Proa |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Stability (fishing)** | Moderate (racing design) | High (fishing platform) |
| **Shallow draft** | 10" | 8" main, 6" ama |
| **Modularity** | Fixed catamaran | 3 configurations |
| **Expedition storage** | Minimal | 12 cu ft in ama |
| **Standing capability** | Trapeze only | Ama + trampoline |
| **Beach launching** | Yes | Yes (better, less draft) |
| **Build complexity** | Fiberglass molds | CNC stitch-and-glue |
| **Open source** | Proprietary | Fully open |
| **Cost** | $8,000+ new | <$3,000 DIY |
