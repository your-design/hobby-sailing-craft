## 📖 How to Use

### Update Your Homepage

File: `index.md`

```yaml
---
layout: home
title: Home
---

## Your Content Here

<div class="feature-grid">
  <!-- Feature cards -->
</div>

<div class="stats-row">
  <!-- Statistics -->
</div>
```

### Create New Pages

```yaml
---
layout: page
title: About
subtitle: Optional subtitle
---

Your page content in markdown...
```

### Customize Colors

Edit `assets/css/main.css`:

```css
:root {
  --ocean-deep: #YOUR_COLOR;
  --coral-accent: #YOUR_COLOR;
}
```
